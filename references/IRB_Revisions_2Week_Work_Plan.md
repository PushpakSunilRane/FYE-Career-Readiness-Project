### A. Revised Survey Consent Form (for Qualtrics first page)

**Copy into Qualtrics consent block:**

---

**Informed Consent to Participate in Research**

**Study Title:** Outcomes in Learner Support Personnel in FYE  
**IRB Protocol Number:** IRB #2022-89  
**Institution:** California State University, Chico (Chico State)

**Principal Investigator (PI):**  
Sue Peterson  
Email: [sepeterson@csuchico.edu](mailto:sepeterson@csuchico.edu)  
Phone: (530) 898-6495

You are invited to take part in a research study conducted at California State University, Chico.

#### Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this study is to learn about the experiences and outcomes of Peer Academic Leaders (PALs) / FYE student employees in the First-Year Experience (FYE) program at Chico State. The survey asks about areas such as academic development, sense of belonging, leadership and communication skills, career readiness, and transferable skills.

This survey is adapted from a mentor-program survey originally developed at Utah Valley University (UVU). This research is conducted by the research team listed above and is **not a university-sponsored requirement**. Taking part is voluntary.

#### Eligibility

You may participate if you meet **all** of the following:

- You are 18 years of age or older; and
- You are a Chico State student; and
- You are currently employed by First-Year Experience at Chico State as a Peer Academic Leader (PAL) / FYE student employee  
*(If former PALs are included in this wave, add: “or you previously served as a PAL / FYE student employee.”)*

#### What You Will Be Asked to Do

If you agree to participate, you will complete an online Qualtrics survey. The survey includes closed-ended questions (such as Likert-scale items), open-ended questions, and demographic/background questions (for example, length of time in the PAL role and current or former status). The survey takes about **20–30 minutes**.

At the end, you may be asked whether you are interested in an optional follow-up interview. If you are interested, a **separate consent form** with interview procedures (including recording) will be provided before any interview begins.

#### Risks

This study involves **minimal risk**. Possible risks include:

- Mild psychological discomfort when reflecting on work or academic experiences; and
- The unlikely risk of a loss of confidentiality / data breach.

#### Benefits

You may not receive a direct personal benefit. Your responses may help improve student employee experiences in FYE and related learner-support roles at Chico State.

#### Confidentiality (Not Anonymous)

Participation is **confidential, but not anonymous**. Identifying information may be collected so the research team can follow up or link responses when needed. Identifying information will be stored separately from survey responses whenever feasible and replaced with unique codes. A master list linking identifiers to codes will be stored securely and accessible only to the principal investigator / authorized research team members.

- Your individual survey responses will **not** be shared with supervisors.
- Identifiable data will **not** be used for employment-related decisions.
- Supervisors will not know who participated unless you choose to tell them.
- Results will be reported only in aggregate or with de-identified quotations.

Data will be stored on password-protected systems accessible only to the research team and managed according to institutional data security policies and IRB requirements.

#### Voluntary Participation

Participation is completely voluntary. You may refuse to participate, skip any question you do not wish to answer, or stop at any time without penalty. Participation or non-participation will have **no impact** on your employment, academic standing, or relationship with the university or FYE program.

#### Survey Response Settings

You may skip any question. If a question appears to require a response in Qualtrics, a **“Prefer not to answer”** or **“No opinion”** option will be available so you can move forward without providing a substantive answer.

#### Academic / FERPA Records (if applicable)

If this study requests access to academic records, that request will be described clearly and will require your consent. Possible academic data may include GPA, academic standing, and enrollment status.  
*(If academic data is NOT collected for the survey-only pathway, delete this paragraph and state: “This survey does not request access to your academic records.”)*

#### Contact Information

- For questions about the study, contact the PI: Sue Peterson, [sepeterson@csuchico.edu](mailto:sepeterson@csuchico.edu), (530) 898-6495.  
- For questions or concerns about your rights as a research participant, contact the Institutional Review Board at California State University, Chico: **[irb@csuchico.edu](mailto:irb@csuchico.edu)** or **(530) 898-3145**.

#### Your Copy of This Consent

Please print or save a copy of this consent form for your records before continuing.

#### Consent

1. You are eligible to participate based on the criteria above;

### C. Skip vs Required Wording + Qualtrics Settings Note

#### Problem IRB flagged

Consent previously said participants must reply to all items **and** could skip items. That is confusing.

#### Final wording to use (consent + survey intro)

Use this consistent language everywhere:

> You may skip any question you do not wish to answer.  
> If a question is set to require a response in Qualtrics so you can continue, a **“Prefer not to answer”** or **“No opinion”** choice will be available.  
> Choosing that option lets you move forward without providing a substantive answer.  
> You may exit the survey at any time without penalty.

#### Recommended Qualtrics setup


| Setting                                                    | Recommendation                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forced response on sensitive/open items                    | Prefer **Off** (true skip)                                        |
| Forced response on scale items (if needed for survey flow) | **On only if** “Prefer not to answer” / “No opinion” is an option |
| Demographic items                                          | Include “Prefer not to answer”                                    |
| Progress / submit                                          | Allow exit anytime; do not penalize incomplete answers            |
| Consent page                                               | Two buttons: “I consent” / “I do not consent”                     |


#### Short note you can upload with IRB materials

**Qualtrics Response Settings Note (IRB #2022-89)**  
Survey questions are finalized. To address IRB feedback about skip vs required responses: participants may skip questions. Where Qualtrics requires selecting an option to advance, items include a “Prefer not to answer” or “No opinion” choice. Consent language has been revised so it no longer states that all items must be answered while also allowing skips.
