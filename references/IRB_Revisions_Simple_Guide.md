# Comment A — Anonymity and supervisor protection

## What IRB asked
- Is the survey anonymous?  
- Or will you collect names to link to other data?  
- If you use a survey link, how will you protect employee responses from possible retribution by supervisors?

## Before
Draft said participation is confidential but not anonymous, and supervisors would not see answers — but IRB still asked for clearer confirmation.

## Use this now (put in consent + recruitment email)

**About anonymity:**  
Participation is **confidential, but not anonymous**. We may collect identifying information (such as name/email) only for follow-up interviews or linking data. Identifying information will be stored separately from survey answers whenever possible.

**About supervisor protection:**  
- Supervisors will **not** have access to individual survey responses.  
- Identifiable data will **not** be used for employment decisions.  
- Supervisors will **not** track or monitor who participates.  
- Choosing to participate or not will **not** affect your employment, academic standing, or relationship with FYE or the university.  
- Results will be reported only in group form or with de-identified quotes.

# Comment C — Upload interview protocol and questions

## What IRB asked
Please upload/attach a copy of your interview protocol and questions for review.

## Before
The protocol described interviews, but no interview guide/questions document was attached.

## Use this now (draft interview protocol to upload)

**Interview Protocol — Outcomes in Learner Support Personnel in FYE (IRB #2022-89)**

**Format:** Semi-structured Zoom interview  
**Length:** about 20–30 minutes  
**Participants:** subset of current and/or former PALs / FYE student employees who consent to follow-up  
**Recording:** Chico State Zoom audio/video recording with consent  
**Note:** A separate interview consent form will be used before the interview starts.

### Opening script
> Thank you for meeting with me. This interview is part of the study “Outcomes in Learner Support Personnel in FYE,” IRB #2022-89. Participation is voluntary. You can skip any question or stop anytime. With your permission, I will record this Zoom interview. Do you consent to being recorded?

### Draft interview questions
1. Please describe your role and main responsibilities as a PAL / FYE student employee.  
2. What skills do you feel you developed most through this role?  
3. Can you share an example of a challenge you faced at work and how you handled it?  
4. How has this role affected your sense of belonging or connection at Chico State?  
5. How has this role affected your academic confidence or academic habits?  
6. In what ways has this role prepared you for leadership or teamwork?  
7. How has this role shaped your career readiness or future job goals?  
8. What transferable skills from FYE do you think you will use after graduation?  
9. What parts of the FYE weekly process (for example meetings, feedback, reflection) helped you grow the most?  
10. If you could improve support for FYE student employees, what would you change?  
11. Is there anything else you want to share about your experience?

### Closing script
> Thank you for your time. Your answers will be kept confidential and reported without your name. Do you have any questions for me?

# Comment E — Skip vs required wording

## What IRB asked
Your consent says participants need to reply to all items **but** can also skip them. This is confusing.
- Will all items require a response in Qualtrics?  
- If yes, add “No opinion” type options on scales.

## Before
Consent mixed “answer everything” and “you may skip.”

## Use this now
You may skip any question you do not wish to answer.  
If a question requires choosing an option to continue in Qualtrics, a **“Prefer not to answer”** or **“No opinion”** choice will be available.  
You may exit the survey at any time without penalty.

## Qualtrics settings note (upload with package if useful)
- Prefer allowing true skips when possible.  
- If forced response is used, always include “Prefer not to answer” / “No opinion.”  
- Consent language and survey settings now match.
